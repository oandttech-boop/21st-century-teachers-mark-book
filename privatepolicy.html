Privacy Policy Framework

The 21ST Century Teachers Markbook Google Sheet add-on is designed specifically as a localized educational processing script engine. We strongly respect user and data isolation architectures.

Data Collection Boundaries

Our processing modules do not harvest, parse, scrape, transmit, or cache student or user records to outside databases or external infrastructure layers. This includes but is not limited to: student identifiers, name datasets, graded matrices, or presence/attendance configurations.

Localized Storage Layers

Every element managed by this add-on remains bound within your structural Google Spreadsheet environment, operating completely under Google's cloud system isolation protocols and permissions boundaries.
